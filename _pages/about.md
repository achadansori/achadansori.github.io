## About Me

I am an R&D Engineer at PT Aldzama, specializing in embedded systems, robotics, and industrial automation. I hold a Bachelor of Applied Science in Electronics Engineering from Politeknik Elektronika Negeri Surabaya (PENS).

My experience spans the full spectrum of robotics engineering, from low-level embedded programming, PCB design, and control systems to computer vision, IoT, and autonomous navigation. I have worked across diverse domains including marine robotics, offshore operations, smart manufacturing, aquaculture technology, and demolition robotics.

I thrive at the intersection of hardware and software, turning prototypes into field-ready solutions. Whether it's reverse-engineering a control system, deploying an AI inspection pipeline on edge devices, or wiring a production panel, I get things done on the ground.

My ikigai is in robotics, where my technical skills, passion, and purpose converge. I am fascinated by how robotics bridges multiple disciplines to create intelligent systems that extend human capabilities beyond physical and cognitive limitations. I aspire to grow as a full-stack robotics engineer, advancing systems that bring meaningful impact to industry and society.
